Story Description

My interactive story takes place in a medieval fantasy world. You the reader are the main character; you are a knight. Your duty is to protect the royal family that resides within the castle.

You are given choices in the story which and based on your decisions your fate might end up different. Some paths are cruel and dangerous while others are laid back.
